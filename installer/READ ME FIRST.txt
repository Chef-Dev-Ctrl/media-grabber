MEDIA GRABBER - download YouTube & Instagram videos, audio and photos
=====================================================================

INSTALL
1. Double-click MediaGrabber-Setup-1.0.0.exe.
2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (the app isn't code-signed).
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. No admin rights needed. It adds a Start menu entry and (optionally) a desktop shortcut.

INSTALL ON A MAC
1. Pick the right file. Apple menu > About This Mac:
     "Chip: Apple M1/M2/M3/M4"  ->  MediaGrabber-<version>-mac-arm64.dmg
     "Processor: Intel"         ->  MediaGrabber-<version>-mac-x86_64.dmg
2. Open the .dmg and drag Media Grabber onto the Applications folder.
3. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the app for malware (it isn't
   from the App Store). Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
   down to "Media Grabber was blocked" and click "Open Anyway". You only do this once.

USE
- Paste a link (Ctrl+V anywhere in the window), pick Video / Audio only / No sound,
  pick a quality, click Download.
- Paste several links at once and use "Download all".
- Everything you download is in the Library tab, and saved to Downloads\MediaGrabber
  (change the folder under Settings).

INSTAGRAM
Instagram only works when logged in. Open Settings (gear icon, top right) > Accounts >
"Log in to Instagram", and sign in in the window that opens. It closes by itself once
you're in. Your password only goes to Instagram.

MAC / IPHONE / ANDROID (OFFICE NETWORK)
One Windows PC can share Media Grabber with everyone on the office network:
Settings > Office network > turn on "Share on office network". It shows an address,
a QR code and a 6-digit PIN. Others open the address in their browser (or scan the QR
code on a phone), enter the PIN, and can download to their own device with
"Save to this device". The sharing PC must stay on with Media Grabber open.
If Windows asks about the firewall, allow "Private networks".

IF DOWNLOADS START FAILING
YouTube and Instagram change things often. Open Settings > "Downloader updates" >
"Check for updates", then "Update now" and "Restart app".
If YouTube says "confirm you're not a bot", wait an hour or add a YouTube login in Settings.

UNINSTALL
Settings > Apps > Media Grabber > Uninstall. Your downloaded files are kept.

Only download content you have the rights to use.
